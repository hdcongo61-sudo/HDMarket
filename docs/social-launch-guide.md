# HDMarket social launch guide

This guide uses social platforms for discovery and sends customers to HDMarket for product details, payment, delivery, and order tracking.

## The launch promise

Use one simple message throughout the launch:

> Find products from local sellers, compare offers, order securely, and get delivery through HDMarket.

Keep the transaction on HDMarket. Social posts should link to one product or one campaign, never to a generic homepage.

## Seven days before launch

Prepare 20–30 products from different categories, price points, and sellers. Prioritize products that are visually clear, available now, easy to explain, and deliverable in the target area.

For each product, prepare three vertical videos:

1. Problem and solution: show the customer problem, then the product solving it.
2. Demonstration: show the product being used in a real setting.
3. Offer and proof: show the price, delivery information, seller, and a review or result.

Make every video vertical, full-screen, easy to understand without sound, and suitable for captions. TikTok’s creative guidance recommends 9:16 video, clear calls to action, audio, and several creative variations. See [TikTok Creative Center guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/creative-tips-for-accessories/pc).

Create a unique campaign link for every combination of product, platform, creator, and campaign. HDMarket’s social share tools and `/s/:socialCode?source=...&campaign=...` links are designed for this.

Prepare a short answer for each product covering price, availability, delivery area, delivery cost, payment options, and the product link.

## Launch week

Publish consistently instead of publishing one large announcement:

- TikTok: two or three videos each day.
- Instagram Reels: one or two videos each day.
- Facebook: one video or product post each day.
- WhatsApp Status: several product posts each day.
- Sellers and creators: at least one personal recommendation for each selected product.

Each post should have one action. Use “Voir le produit sur HDMarket” for a product page or “Écrivez-nous sur WhatsApp” for a conversation. Do not use multiple competing calls to action in the same post.

## Content formats to rotate

- “Voici le problème que ce produit règle.”
- “Test réel en 20 secondes.”
- “Trois raisons de choisir ce produit.”
- Before and after.
- Unboxing or customer review.
- Product comparison.
- Seller behind the scenes.
- “Ce que vous recevez pour ce prix.”
- Local delivery demonstration.
- Weekly deal or limited stock message.

Put the product and the benefit in the opening seconds. Add the price or a clear way to request it, the delivery area, and the HDMarket action in on-screen text. TikTok’s ecommerce guidance highlights demonstrations, problem-and-solution videos, before-and-after content, creators, and clear offers or calls to action. See [TikTok conversion guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-that-drives-conversions).

## Creator plan

Start with five to ten local micro-creators rather than one expensive account. Give each creator:

- a unique HDMarket campaign link;
- one or two products to demonstrate;
- a short list of facts they must not change;
- permission to use their own language and style;
- a fixed fee, a verified-order bonus, or both.

Pay attention to product-page visits, conversations, and orders. Follower count and views alone do not prove sales.

## WhatsApp conversion flow

Use this journey:

`TikTok / Instagram / Facebook → WhatsApp or product page → HDMarket checkout`

Reply with the current product facts from HDMarket. Do not promise stock, price, delivery time, or payment completion from an old social post. Send the customer to the current HDMarket product page before checkout.

The Social Commerce Hub supports WhatsApp, Instagram Messaging, and Facebook Messenger connectors, trackable links, attribution, and seller analytics. Enable the channels only after provider credentials, webhooks, privacy notices, and test conversations are ready. Meta’s commerce policy places responsibility for transaction terms, privacy, taxes, and applicable requirements on the business. See [WhatsApp commerce policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy/preview?lang=es_LA).

## Paid promotion

Use the first five to seven days to test organic content. Promote only videos that already generate product clicks, saves, shares, or conversations.

Test three audiences:

1. A broad local audience.
2. People interested in the product category.
3. People who visited a product page or started a conversation.

Start with a small budget. Stop campaigns that produce views without product activity. Increase budget gradually for campaigns that produce verified orders, and keep separate campaign links so acquisition cost can be compared.

## 30-day schedule

### Days 1–7: test

Publish the prepared videos across TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp. Record which opening, format, seller, and product produce clicks and conversations.

### Days 8–14: amplify

Boost the best three to five videos. Start creator posts and use the same product links so their results remain attributable.

### Days 15–21: retarget

Show related products and proof to people who clicked, viewed, added to cart, or contacted the business. Follow up through approved messaging flows.

### Days 22–30: repeat winners

Create new versions of the best formats, replace weak products, publish customer proof, and review cost per verified order.

## Weekly dashboard

Review these metrics by platform, seller, product, creator, and campaign:

- reach and video completion;
- saves and shares;
- product-page clicks;
- WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger conversations;
- add-to-cart rate;
- checkout starts;
- completed orders;
- cost per completed order;
- creator conversion rate.

Use HDMarket’s server-confirmed attribution and order data for revenue decisions. Likes and page views are useful diagnostic signals, but they are not sales.

## Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] Products have current prices, stock, delivery areas, and usable photos or videos.
- [ ] Every post has a product-specific or campaign-specific link.
- [ ] Seller and creator links identify their source and campaign.
- [ ] WhatsApp replies contain current product facts and a checkout link.
- [ ] Meta credentials and webhook signatures are configured before enabling live processing.
- [ ] Social feature flags remain in test rollout until a real conversation and order are verified.
- [ ] Consent, privacy, returns, delivery, and payment information are visible on HDMarket.
- [ ] A daily owner is assigned to answer conversations and review failed links.
